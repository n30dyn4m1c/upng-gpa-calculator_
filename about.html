About

Calculate your UPNG GPA & CGPA.

Features & Purpose

This tool calculates your UPNG GPA and CGPA using the correct formula. Course data is sourced from official listings, and credit values are filled in automatically. Courses not in the list can be entered manually. Repeat courses are handled correctly — only the highest grade counts.

The result you see here is the result you'd get from the student system.

Everything runs in your browser — no grades or personal data are sent anywhere.
