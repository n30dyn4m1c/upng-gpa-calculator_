About this tool

Built for UPNG students, by one of them.

A free, private GPA and CGPA calculator that uses the university's own grading scale and course list — so the number you see here is the number you would get from the student system.

What it does

The correct formula

Credit-weighted grade points over total credits, on the official UPNG five-point scale.

Real course data

Search the official course list by code or title and credits fill themselves in. Anything missing can be typed by hand.

Repeats handled properly

On the CGPA page a repeated course only counts once, using the highest grade you attained.

Private by design

Everything runs in your browser. Your grades are saved on your own device and never sent anywhere.

Feedback & suggestions

Any feedback is helpful, and changes get made according to what users say. If something is wrong or missing, or if you would like a new feature, say so.
